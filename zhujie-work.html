PROJECT 01 · 2022-2024

Star Wonderland

Pop-up book and illustration

An illustrated handmade book with folded spaces, bold colour and a small star character connecting the scenes.

Overview

I explored how a book could become a series of small three-dimensional scenes. My focus was the reading experience: how the pages open, how the images connect, and how a simple star character leads the eye through different settings.

Audience

Readers exploring an illustrated art book

My role

Independent project: concept, design development and presentation

Creative process

I sketched the cover and the folded page structure before building the book. I developed each scene with a different colour palette, then combined painted surfaces with cut paper and other materials. The construction photographs show the work at different stages, including the folds and the central opening.

Final design

The finished book brings together geometric panels, a wave scene and softer organic shapes. Repeating the star character helps the different pages belong to the same world. The photographs show the actual book both open and held in the hand.

What I learned

The strongest part is the change between a flat image and a small space. I would refine the joins and photograph every spread against the same background so the sequence is easier to follow.

PROJECT 02 · 2022-2024

Suzhou Brunch

Spatial concept and visual presentation

A restaurant concept that connects Suzhou food culture with courtyards, paths and a group of small buildings.

Overview

I proposed a brunch restaurant inspired by Suzhou gardens and local food culture. The concept considers young diners and combines shared dining with more private spaces. My aim was to connect the restaurant experience with a sense of place.

Audience

Diners interested in Suzhou food and local culture

My role

Independent project: concept, design development and presentation

Creative process

My mind map brought together the menu, courtyard, circulation and different dining spaces. I studied table and aisle dimensions, drew the building forms and developed a plan. I then used perspective drawings, digital views and a physical model to communicate the proposal.

Final design

The proposal arranges small buildings around outdoor space. A circular pavilion gives the composition a focal point, while the paths connect dining areas. The model photographs show the arrangement from above and at eye level. The interior image explains the relationship between diners, light and the courtyard.

What I learned

This project helped me explain an idea through several kinds of image. I would simplify the presentation boards and make the labels more consistent. That experience is useful when I organise information in a graphic design project.

PROJECT 03 · 2022-2024

Experimental Printmaking

Screen printing and material studies

A set of studio studies using repeated marks, texture, handmade stamps and overprinted colour.

Overview

I explored different ways to build an image through print. The work includes texture tests, carved stamps and a screen print. I was interested in how the same drawing could change through colour, layering and the pressure of a handmade process.

Audience

Viewers of experimental prints and illustrated surfaces

My role

Independent project: concept, design development and presentation

Creative process

I began with material tests and small motifs. For the screen print, I prepared the screen, applied the coating and washed out the image. My first attempt did not give a clear result. I recorded the problems and made a second attempt before comparing the colour combinations.

Final design

The test sheet records several versions of the same image. Yellow creates a lighter result, while the green version gives the forms more weight. Overlapping colours introduce new shapes and make each print slightly different. The texture and stamp studies show other ways of making marks by hand.

Display concept

What I learned

The failed attempt is a useful part of this project because it shows what I changed. I would keep clearer notes on each print and compare the results in a more controlled sequence. I want to use this interest in texture in future poster work.

PROJECT 04 · 2022-2024

Stray Cat Shelter

Modular concept and cardboard prototype

A shelter study that develops a geometric composition into connected spaces for climbing and resting.

Overview

I developed a shelter concept in response to stray cats around my school. I explored enclosed spaces, raised platforms and connections between geometric modules. The project considers how cats could climb and rest, and how the shelter could sit within a campus setting.

Audience

Cats as users, with a campus community around the proposed shelter

My role

Independent project: concept, design development and presentation

Creative process

I used a mind map to organise the problem and studied the relationship between the modules and a cat's movements. Mondrian's geometric compositions informed the arrangement. I developed sketches and dimensioned elevations, then made a 1:2 cardboard prototype with openings and different levels.

Final design

The prototype makes the openings and routes between the modules visible. The photographs document assembly and a cat exploring the model. I also made a photomontage of the proposed campus setting to communicate its scale and context. This remains a design proposal.

What I learned

Making the prototype helped me move beyond the appearance of the drawing and examine the space inside it. I would improve the joints and develop a suitable material and maintenance plan before considering an outdoor version.
