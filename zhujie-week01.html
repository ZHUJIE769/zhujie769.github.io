Reflection

Draft for review.

I wanted my homepage to look playful and personal. I used a pink background, a large portfolio title and simple menu labels to make the main information stand out. I think the design shows the cute and lively feeling I wanted. Some small text could be darker, and the decoration near Contact could be simpler. If I continue developing the page, I would check the text size on a phone and make the navigation easier to read.

Hourly rate calculation

(SGD 1,000 target monthly payment + SGD 155 overhead + SGD 125 reserve) / 40 billable hours = SGD 32 per hour. Operating costs are already included in this rate and are not charged again as an extra overhead line.

Hourly versus project-based

Hourly pricing suits work with an uncertain amount of corrections. A fixed project price suits a clear scope. This sample covers one A5 flyer, one design direction, two revision rounds and a final PDF. New sizes, new concepts or extra work would need a revised quotation.

Reflection

Draft for review.

From this example, I can see that design work includes research, revisions and file preparation as well as the final image. The SGD 32 hourly rate already includes running costs, so I should not add them again. I also need to explain the project size, files and number of revisions clearly. I am still learning, so I would record my time on future projects and use it to make my estimates more accurate.
