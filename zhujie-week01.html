Exercise 01A: Communication

Date: October 1, 2026

Research

  • Rumana Akter — Personal Portfolio Website

    The soft pink and white palette is close to the gentle feeling I want. The project separates the hero, about and contact sections clearly. My homepage uses a larger title, more texture and pixel-style menu lettering, so it feels less formal.

  • Eva Muñoz Rubio — NEO BRUTALISM

    This typography project combines heavy block lettering with flowing calligraphy and soft pink tones. That contrast relates to the large letters and decorative P in my homepage. I want to keep the playful contrast while making the smaller menu labels clear.

Process Work

I want my homepage to introduce me as someone with ideas who is cute, lively and serious about design. My Canva layout uses a pink textured background, an outlined title and pixel-style navigation.

  • My name is the largest element, so it is the first thing to notice.
  • The decorative P adds a softer shape beside the bold letters.
  • Home, Work, About and Contact suggest the main sections.
  • The black Learn More button gives the page a clear next action.

Final Outcome

Why I want to be a graphic designer

I want to create eye-catching designs that inspire fresh ideas and reflect my playful, thoughtful personality.

View my homepage design in Canva

Reflection

I wanted my homepage to look playful and personal. I think the pink background and large title show the cute and lively feeling I wanted. Some small text could be darker, and the decoration near Contact could be simpler. I want the page to look interesting while still being easy to read on a phone.

Exercise 01B: Costing

Date: October 1, 2026

Research

  • Jabin Tasnim — Modern & Professional Invoice Design

    This project focuses on clear billing information and a simple hierarchy. For my costing exercise, the client, item descriptions and total need to be easy to find.

  • Adobe Express — Invoice Guide

    The guide covers the information an invoice needs, including the invoice number, client, date, services and total. It supports the document structure; my SGD figures are an exercise calculation.

Process Work

I am learning Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva. I want to develop poster design, typography and layout first. The costing exercise uses an A5 flyer for the pie room, with one design direction and two revision rounds.

Reflection

I can see that design work includes research, revisions and file preparation as well as the final image. I need to explain the project size, files and number of revisions clearly. I am still learning, so I would record my time on future projects to make my estimates more accurate.

My Weekly Journal

I like design because it can make people stop and look. For my homepage, I wanted people to see that I am playful and have my own ideas. The pink background feels like me, and the large title makes my name easy to notice. I also need to think about how small text and menu labels work together.

Costing is a different side of design. A simple flyer still includes research, layout, changes and preparing files. I am learning Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva, so I want to be honest about my current level. A clear price and a clear scope would help me avoid promising more than I can deliver.
