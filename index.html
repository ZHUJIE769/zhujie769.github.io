A little about me

Thoughtful.
Playful.
My own person.

I want to make work that catches the eye and opens the door to a new idea.

I’m studying graphic design at PSB Academy and expect to graduate in 2028. Poster design is where my curiosity feels most at home. I’m learning Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva, and hope to work in a creative studio.

Creativity, patience and optimism guide the designer I want to become. I’m independent and determined, with room to explore more than one style.
