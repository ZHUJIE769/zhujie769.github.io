Exercise 03B: Promotions

Date: October 12, 2026

This exercise is about introducing a partner and improving my own introduction.

Research

The brief asks us to spend an hour learning about each other, prepare a 30-second introduction independently, introduce the partner to the class, and then rewrite our own introduction.

The classroom notes and the partner details below were supplied by zhujie. liyu enjoys poster and logo design, is careful, patient and friendly, and hopes to become a graphic designer.

Process Work

Part 1 — About Me

My name is zhujie. I am studying Graphic Design and Media. My hobbies are playing games and sleeping. I am good at cooking and want to improve my 3D modelling and design skills. I am introverted, friendly, and kind. I am proud of being independent. I have overcome the challenges of living alone and communicating in different languages. In the future, I hope to become a famous visual designer and create memorable designs.

Part 2 — How Others See Me

My partner thinks I am easy to get along with and describes me as cute, clever, and kind. They think I could improve my design skills and spoken English.

Changes to make

  • Shorten the wording so the main points are easier to follow.
  • Keep independence and the future visual design goal as the main personal points.
  • Mention design, 3D modelling and spoken English as skills to improve.

Final Outcome

My revised self-introduction / draft

Hi, I’m zhujie, a Graphic Design and Media student. I am introverted, friendly and kind, and proud of being independent. Living alone has helped me handle challenges and communicate across languages. I want to improve my design, 3D modelling and spoken English. My goal is to become a visual designer who creates memorable work.

Introducing liyu / 30-second introduction draft

Hello everyone. My partner is liyu, a Graphic Design and Media student. She enjoys poster design and logo design. She is careful, patient and friendly. In the future, she hopes to become a graphic designer. Her attention to detail and patience are qualities she can bring to her design work.

This introduction uses the partner details supplied by zhujie. The target is 30 seconds; read it aloud and adjust the pace. Actual classroom presentation timing is not documented here.

Reflection

Draft for review.

My partner described me as cute, clever and kind, which gives another view of my personality. Their comments also helped identify design skills and spoken English as areas to improve. In the revised introduction, I kept my independence and future goal, and made the wording shorter and clearer. I want to become more confident when explaining who I am and what I can do.
