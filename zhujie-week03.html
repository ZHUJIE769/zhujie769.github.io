03

Final Outcome

  1. 2028

    Build the foundation

    Graduate, refine a focused portfolio and apply for junior graphic design roles.

    Action: Finish self-directed poster studies; practise explaining decisions.

  2. 2029–2030

    Learn inside a studio

    Develop reliable production, typography and collaborative habits.

    Action: Seek feedback; observe briefs, revisions, client communication and handover.

  3. 2031–2032

    Grow a point of view

    Take more responsibility for poster, campaign and identity projects.

    Action: Learn art direction fundamentals and strengthen research and presentation.

  4. 2033–2035

    Lead with care

    Aim for a senior designer role if ability and opportunities support it.

    Action: Guide small projects, mentor newer designers and collaborate across disciplines.

  5. 2036–2038

    Choose the next chapter

    Consider design leadership, independent practice or a small collaborative studio.

    Action: Review financial readiness, team skills and the type of work worth pursuing.

04

Reflection

Draft note Reflection starter to rewrite after your actual work. This is not evidence of an activity already completed.

The value of a long plan is the next practical step it makes visible. After researching actual roles, name the biggest gap between your current skills and the first role you want. Review the plan regularly, and adjust it when experience changes your interests or opportunities.

Exercise 03B

Promotions

October 12, 2026Learning draft

Discover the difference between how someone sees you and how you introduce yourself.

01

Research

The course brief sets a paired activity: one hour to learn about each other, an independently prepared 30-second partner introduction to the class and lecturer, then a rewritten self-introduction. A partner must not help write the independent introduction.

The name liyu is used with zhujie’s permission. No actual interview answers, presentation record or colleague observations have been supplied.

Process Work

Proposed questions

  • What kind of design do you enjoy, and why?
  • Which three qualities describe your approach?
  • What would you like to improve?
  • Where would you like your design work to take you?

Simulated editing feedback

Name poster design earlier, explain the intended effect of the work, and keep the software list brief. These are sample editing suggestions, not observations attributed to a real conversation.

03

Final Outcome

Partner introduction / fictional practice / 54 words

This is liyu, a design student who enjoys illustration and layout. In this practice profile, she is observant, patient and open-minded. She likes finding visual ideas in everyday details and hopes to work in a creative studio. Her next goal is to strengthen her typography skills and build confidence when presenting her design choices.

Aim for 30 seconds; actual timing still needs a spoken rehearsal.

Self-introduction / before draft

Hi, I’m zhujie. I’m a graphic design student, and I enjoy making creative work. I’m learning Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva. I hope to work in a creative studio after graduation. I want my work to feel playful and show my personality.

Self-introduction / revised draft

Hi, I’m zhujie, a graphic design student drawn to poster design and expressive typography. I want to create eye-catching work that sparks fresh ideas. I’m developing my skills in Illustrator, Photoshop and Canva. My approach is playful, independent and thoughtful, and I hope to bring that energy to a creative studio after graduating in 2028.

04

Reflection

Draft note Reflection starter to rewrite after your actual work. This is not evidence of an activity already completed.

After the real activity, compare a colleague’s actual observation with your own description. Explain which point felt accurate and which change made the introduction more specific. Replace the simulated feedback and profile before recording the exercise as completed.
