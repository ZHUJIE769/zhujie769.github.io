Exercise 03A: Future Gazing

Date: October 12, 2026

A proposed ten-year direction, from an expected 2028 graduation to 2038.

Research

  • Adobe Learn — Illustrator ↗

    A practical source for building current software and visual skills. Choose specific lessons and keep records of your learning.

  • Pentagram — Work ↗

    Use studio projects to explore possible disciplines and the skills behind varied design work.

  • D&AD New Blood Awards ↗

    A reference for student and emerging creative briefs. Future participation is a possible goal, not an award already achieved.

Add your own notes, screenshots and actual visit dates.

Process Work

To add: This is a future plan, not a prediction or a list of completed achievements.

Current positionDevelopment needFirst proposed action
Learning Illustrator, Photoshop and CanvaMore confident typography and file preparationComplete focused studies and compare the results.
Interest in postersA coherent body of personal workDevelop a small poster series with written rationale.
Independent and determinedCollaboration and clear presentationPractise receiving feedback and explaining decisions.

The brief suggests optional themes including further study, training, job titles, awards, income and social contribution. No salary, award, employment or client is invented here. A community poster project could become a future contribution once the relevant skills and permission are in place.

Final Outcome

  1. 2028

    Build the foundation

    Graduate, refine a focused portfolio and apply for junior graphic design roles.

    Action: Finish self-directed poster studies; practise explaining decisions.

  2. 2029–2030

    Learn inside a studio

    Develop reliable production, typography and collaborative habits.

    Action: Seek feedback; observe briefs, revisions, client communication and handover.

  3. 2031–2032

    Grow a point of view

    Take more responsibility for poster, campaign and identity projects.

    Action: Learn art direction fundamentals and strengthen research and presentation.

  4. 2033–2035

    Lead with care

    Aim for a senior designer role if ability and opportunities support it.

    Action: Guide small projects, mentor newer designers and collaborate across disciplines.

  5. 2036–2038

    Choose the next chapter

    Consider design leadership, independent practice or a small collaborative studio.

    Action: Review financial readiness, team skills and the type of work worth pursuing.

Reflection

Write after completing the work.

  • What worked well?
  • What did I change, and why?
  • What would I improve next time?

Exercise 03B: Promotions

Date: October 12, 2026

This exercise is about introducing a partner and improving my own introduction.

Research

The brief asks us to spend an hour learning about each other, prepare a 30-second introduction independently, introduce the partner to the class, and then rewrite our own introduction.

The two classroom notes below were provided by zhujie. The extra details in the suggested introduction of liyu still need confirmation.

Process Work

Part 1 — About Me

My name is zhujie. I am studying Graphic Design and Media. My hobbies are playing games and sleeping. I am good at cooking and want to improve my 3D modelling and design skills. I am introverted, friendly, and kind. I am proud of being independent. I have overcome the challenges of living alone and communicating in different languages. In the future, I hope to become a famous visual designer and create memorable designs.

Part 2 — How Others See Me

My partner thinks I am easy to get along with and describes me as cute, clever, and kind. They think I could improve my design skills and spoken English.

Changes to make

  • Keep the spoken introduction short and clear.
  • Include independence as a personal strength.
  • Mention design, 3D modelling and spoken English as skills to improve.

Final Outcome

My revised self-introduction / draft

Hi, I’m zhujie, a Graphic Design and Media student. I am introverted, friendly and kind, and proud of being independent. Living alone has helped me handle challenges and communicate across languages. I want to improve my design, 3D modelling and spoken English. My goal is to become a visual designer who creates memorable work.

Introducing liyu / suggested wording

This is liyu, a Graphic Design and Media student. This suggested profile describes her as friendly, patient and observant, with an interest in illustration and layout. She would like to improve her typography and presentation skills and work in a creative studio. These details still need to be checked with her before this introduction is final.

Partner details are provisional. Confirm them with liyu, rewrite the script independently and practise the 30-second timing.
