Final Outcome

Personal Brand Statement 75 words / draft

I am zhujie, a graphic design student interested in poster design. I like designs that catch people’s attention and give them new ideas. I am creative, patient and optimistic. I also have my own opinions and like trying different styles. My brand, Soft Chaos, uses pink, cream and cherry wine with simple type and small handmade details. I hope to work in a creative studio after graduating in 2028 and keep improving my design skills.

Brand Rationale 116 words / draft

I chose Soft Chaos because I like soft colours, but I also want to try bold ideas. The cream background makes the pages comfortable to read. Pink adds a playful feeling, while cherry wine helps headings stand out. I use Arial because it is simple and easy to work with while I am still learning. Small stars and collage ideas can be explored in my own drawings later. I do not want every project to look the same, so the brand gives me a starting point rather than a fixed rule. This direction connects with my creativity, patience and optimism. It also gives me a clear way to introduce myself when I apply to creative studios.

To add: The statement, rationale and visual system are learning drafts. Rewrite and develop them through your own original work.
