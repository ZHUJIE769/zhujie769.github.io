Final Outcome

Personal Brand Statement 78 words / draft

I am zhujie, an emerging graphic designer with a particular interest in poster design. I want to create eye-catching work that sparks fresh ideas and makes people pause. My approach combines creativity, patience and optimism with an independent point of view. Through Soft Chaos, I explore playful typography, collage and a balance between softness and energy. I hope to grow within creative studios while staying open to different visual directions rather than being defined by a single style.

Brand Rationale 123 words / draft

Soft Chaos expresses the balance I want in my design identity: a soft heart and an independent, energetic imagination. Cream and soft pink create warmth, while cherry wine gives the system a stronger voice and supports readable contrast. A restrained serif wordmark introduces a personal tone, paired with a simple sans serif for practical information. Collage, hand-drawn stars and unexpected changes in scale suggest curiosity and play. Clear spacing keeps those experiments from overwhelming the message. The direction reflects my creativity, patience and optimism without requiring every project to look the same. It can support my interest in poster design and give my portfolio a recognisable presence when approaching creative studios. The identity remains a draft to test through original sketches and applications.

Draft note The statement, rationale and visual system are learning drafts. Rewrite and develop them through your own original work.

04

Reflection

Draft note Reflection starter to rewrite after your actual work. This is not evidence of an activity already completed.

A personal brand should be recognisable while leaving room to grow. After testing the identity, explain how the colours, type and mark reflect your values and whether the system still feels like you across different projects. Support the reflection with a real sketch or application change.
