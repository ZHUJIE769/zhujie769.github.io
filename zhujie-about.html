A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious, patient & a little playful.

My name is zhujie, and I am studying Graphic Design and Media at PSB Academy. I enjoy poster design because a good image can catch attention and give people new ideas. I want my work to feel lively, thoughtful and full of personality.

My interest in design started before my current course. During high school, I explored landscape and architectural design through projects such as Suzhou Brunch and a shelter for stray cats. I made drawings, developed spatial ideas and built physical models. These projects taught me to consider scale and how different parts work together. I also experimented with a pop-up book, printing and other handmade work.

I later studied Architecture & Landscape at NAFA in Singapore from 2023 to 2024. I continued learning through design projects and hands-on making. In a group shadow-puppet film project, I contributed to character construction, with each member making individual characters for the shared film. I now want to bring that curiosity into graphic design, especially posters and visual identity.

I describe myself as creative, patient and optimistic. I am independent and like forming my own opinions. Living alone and communicating in different languages have also helped me become more self-reliant. I want to become more confident when explaining my ideas and responding to feedback.

My personal brand is zhujie. Its concept, Soft Chaos, combines soft pink, cream and cherry wine with playful details. The slogan is Soft heart. Wild ideas. I like a cute, friendly feeling, but I also enjoy trying unexpected combinations. I want to keep exploring different styles as my skills develop.

I have used SketchUp, Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop and InDesign in my design learning, and I also use Canva. I am continuing to improve my layout and image-making skills. My earlier spatial work gives me another way to think about composition, structure and how people experience a design.

I expect to graduate in 2028. My next goal is to become a junior graphic designer in a creative studio, learn from experienced designers and build a strong portfolio. In the longer term, I hope to develop my own design studio.
